# English voiceover script

配音版沿用原视频的 41 秒画面、画内字幕和快捷键说明边界；旁白按功能段精简表达，不逐字重复全部字幕。无背景音乐。

| 时间 | English narration |
| --- | --- |
| 00–04 s | Open Tab Station from Chrome. |
| 04–08 s | Or use the shortcut for your system. |
| 08–11 s | All your tabs, in one workspace. |
| 11–14 s | See tabs across your browser windows. |
| 14–17 s | Find any tab with a quick search. |
| 17–20 s | Preview which duplicates stay or close. |
| 20–23 s | Remove duplicates, keeping pinned tabs. |
| 23–26 s | Save a page for later. |
| 26–29 s | Open it, and clear the saved item. |
| 29–32 s | Save a window for future use. |
| 32–35 s | Restore your workspace when you need it. |
| 35–38 s | Your saved window stays available to reuse. |
| 38–41 s | Tab Station. Your tabs, in your hands. |

配音服务：阿里云百炼；模型 `qwen-audio-3.0-tts-plus`，音色 `longanlufeng`，英文。分段合成后按原画面时间轴对齐，少量片段适度加速（最大约 1.126 倍），合成音轨为 48 kHz 单声道 WAV，峰值控制为 −1.5 dB。视频使用 AAC 音轨，原视频画面直接复用。

文件：[配音版视频](tab-station-promo-en-voiced.mp4)、[旁白音轨](tab-station-voiceover-en.wav)、[旁白字幕](tab-station-promo-en-voiced.srt)。
